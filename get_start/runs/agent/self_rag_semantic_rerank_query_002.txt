It had a total of 14 Oscar nominations (tying the record set by the 1950 drama _[All About Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All\\_About\\_Eve \"All About Eve\")_) and won 11 (tying the record set by the 1959 epic historical drama _[Ben-Hur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Hur\\_(1959\\_film) \"Ben-Hur (1959 film)\")_).[[16]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-18) Cameron also won the [Golden Globe Award for Best Director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Director \"Golden Globe Award for Best Director\") and shared the [Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture– Drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Globe_Award_for_Best_Motion_Picture_%E2%80%93_Drama \"Golden Globe Award for Best Motion Picture – Drama\") with the other producers.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-19) He followed this by directing, and producing two underwater documentaries: _[Ghosts of the Abyss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts\\_of\\_the\\_Abyss \"Ghosts of the Abyss\")_ (2003), and _[Aliens of the Deep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliens\\_of\\_the\\_Deep \"Aliens of the Deep\")_ (2005). He returned to directing features in 2009 with the 3D science fiction film _[Avatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar\\_(2009\\_film) \"Avatar (2009 film)\")_. It grossed over $2.9 billion at the worldwide box-office and became the highest grossing of all time surpassing _Titanic_.[[c]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-22)[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-high-16)[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-23)_Avatar_ was nominated for nine Academy Awards and won three in technical categories.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-24) Cameron also earned a second Golden Globe Award for Best Director, and Best Motion Picture– Drama.[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-25) He followed this by executive producing two 3D films, _[Sanctum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctum\\_(film) \"Sanctum (film)\")_ (2011) and _[Cirque du Soleil: Worlds Away](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cirque\\_du\\_Soleil:\\_Worlds\\_Away \"Cirque du Soleil: Worlds Away\")_ (2012), as well as the documentary _[Deepsea Challenge 3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepsea\\_Challenger \"Deepsea Challenger\")_ (2014). Cameron directed _[Avatar: The Way of Water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:\\_The\\_Way\\_of\\_Water \"Avatar: The Way of Water\")_ (2022), a sequel to _[Avatar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar\\_(2009\\_film) \"Avatar (2009 film)\")_ (2009), which was the highest-grossing film of the year with over $2.3 billion at the worldwide box-office. It received generally positive reviews and was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Picture and won the [Academy Award for Best Visual Effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Visual_Effects \"Academy Award for Best Visual Effects\").[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-26)[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-27)[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-28) A third film in the _Avatar_ series, _[Avatar: Fire and Ash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar:\\_Fire\\_and\\_Ash \"Avatar: Fire and Ash\")_, was released on December 19, 2025.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-Ava2-29)\n\nCameron made his television debut in 1998 playing himself in the sitcom _[Mad About You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad\\_About\\_You \"Mad About You\")_. Two years later, he executive produced the science fiction television series _[Dark Angel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark\\_Angel\\_(2000\\_TV\\_series) \"Dark Angel (2000 TV series)\")_ (2000) starring [Jessica Alba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Alba \"Jessica Alba\"). In 2005, he appeared in two documentaries about the [sinking of the RMS Titanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinking_of_the_RMS_Titanic \"Sinking of the RMS Titanic\"): _Last Mysteries of the Titanic_, and _Tony Robinson's Titanic Adventure_. He also made appearances as himself on the comedy-drama television series _[Entourage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entourage\\_(U.S.\\_TV\\_series) \"Entourage (U.S. TV series)\")_ that same year. Cameron followed this by executive producing two biblical documentaries, _[The Exodus Decoded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The\\_Exodus\\_Decoded \"The Exodus Decoded\")_ (2006) and _[Lost Tomb of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost\\_Tomb\\_of\\_Jesus \"Lost Tomb of Jesus\")_ (2007). He executive produced and appeared in a third Titanic related documentary, _Titanic: Final Word with James Cameron_, in 2012. Two years later, Cameron executive produced the climate change documentary television series _[Years of Living Dangerously](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Years\\_of\\_Living\\_Dangerously \"Years of Living Dangerously\")_ (2014) which received the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Documentary or Nonfiction Series.[[27]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-30)\n\n## Film\n\n[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=James_Cameron_filmography&action=edit&section=1 \"Edit section: Film\")]\n\nKey †Denotes films that have not yet been released\n\n### Feature films\n\n[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=James_Cameron_filmography&action=edit&section=2 \"Edit section: Feature films\")]\n\n| Year | Title | Credited as | Notes | Ref(s) |\n| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |\n| Director | Writer | Producer | Editor |\n| 1978 | _[Xenogenesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xenogenesis\\_(film) \"Xenogenesis (film)\")_ | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | Short film Co-directed and co-written with [Randall Frakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randall_Frakes \"Randall Frakes\") Also visual effects producer | [[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-pir-1) |\n| 1982 | _[Piranha II: The Spawning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piranha\\_II:\\_The\\_Spawning \"Piranha II: The Spawning\")_ | Yes | Yes[[d]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cameron_filmography#cite_note-31) | No | No | Co-written with [Charles H.
